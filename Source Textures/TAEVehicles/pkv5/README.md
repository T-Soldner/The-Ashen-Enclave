# House Karr PK-V5 Paint

Original exterior model/textures: Kharak. Arma integration: Hataaylir.
Based on the installed PK-V5 MMTS Republic no-insignia exterior textures.
Gold is sampled from the existing TAE V-wing paint; neutral panels are darkened
without resizing or replacing the original weathering and surface detail.

Reproduce with tools/recolor-pkv5.py after locally converting the six installed
Republic no-insignia PAA textures to docs/local-assets/pkv5-original-png and
placing a converted TAE V-wing main texture there as vwing-gold-reference.png.
Convert these output PNGs to TAEVehicles/data/pkv5/*.paa with Pal2PacE.
Models, interior, normal maps, materials, damage textures and wrecks remain
provided by the required upstream PK-V5 mod. No models are redistributed here.
