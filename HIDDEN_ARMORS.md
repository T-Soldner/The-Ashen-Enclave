# Hidden Armor Tally

These custom armor classes are intentionally hidden from normal, Curator,
Extended, and restricted arsenals.

| Armor | Classname | Wearable behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frenk's Arbiter Armor | `tae_frenk_armor` | Not enabled; Frenk's wearable applies standard Acklay armor |
| Jimothy's Armor | `tae_jimothy_armor` | Not enabled; Jimothy's wearable applies standard Acklay armor |
| Nox's Armor | `tae_nox_armor` | Not enabled; Nox's wearable applies Mandalorian Apprentice armor |
