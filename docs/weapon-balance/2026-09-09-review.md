# TAE Arsenal / Quartermaster Balance Review

Date: 2026-09-09. Read-only source review of the current arsenal and QM, using installed LS Stable, not Public Testing. No weapon or arsenal changes made.

## Scope and confidence

This is a configuration-level review, NOT a measured time-to-kill leaderboard. Main infantry weapon families were traced through installed third-party configs, magazine/ammo inheritance and selected Kandosii scripts. Protected LFP configs could not be resolved; LW-7 and Westar Sniper remain unranked. This is not a complete engine-merged config dump: other loaded addons, magazine wells, scripted effects, ACE settings and firing animations may change the observed result. Missing values below mean unresolved, not zero. CfgPatches dependency ordering was not fully simulated by the temporary parser; shortlisted findings were cross-checked against their defining configs.

`hit` is nominal engine damage, not player health lost. `caliber` is a penetration coefficient, not millimeters. Projectile impact speed versus typicalSpeed, explosive coefficient, armor and hit location matter. Several JMS projectiles launch below their typicalSpeed; several IDA projectiles have explosive=1 with no splash damage. Do not compare them using hit multiplied by RPM as an actual damage-per-second estimate. See [Bohemia damage documentation](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_3_Damage_Description) and [weapon config reference](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/CfgWeapons_Config_Reference).

## Main findings

1. **NT-242 is the strongest clear sniper outlier to test.** Its allowed precision magazine has hit=100, caliber=40, 15 rounds; cycle 2 seconds, dispersion=0.000075. IQA/DC-15X/Galaar-90 use hit=25, caliber=2.2, five rounds; cycle 0.6 seconds, dispersion=0.0001. NT's slower follow-up and 800 m/s projectile versus the IDA trio's 3200 m/s are real disadvantages, but its penetration advantage is enormous. Its weapon mass is only 50 versus the IQA's 120. Do not interpret indirectHit=35 with indirectHitRange=0 as a 35-damage area blast.

2. **Rifleman has specialist capability hidden in ordinary weapons.** EE-2 HighPower substitutes knd_mag_EE2_HP, costs five ordinary rounds, and uses a projectile inheriting B_19mm_HE. The KND gadgets script performs the mode/magazine switch; merely excluding the hidden magazine from the arsenal does not establish a restriction. Westar-35S also has an underbarrel scatter muzzle and the arsenal includes its scatter cell. Decide whether these capabilities are permitted under Rifleman before changing damage.

3. **Bowcaster is a powerful Grenadier pick beyond its explosive role.** The normal 20-round cell uses hit=45, caliber=4 and the weapon cycles at 0.26 seconds (~231 RPM theoretical semi-auto limit), with 0.00025 dispersion. It also has the explicitly permitted two-round heavy cell: hit=55, indirectHit=12, indirectHitRange=6, explosive=0.6. Its mass=275 is a substantial disadvantage, but stamina-disabling mods can soften that disadvantage. It deserves direct comparison with sniper weapons as well as grenade launchers.

4. **Rancor is the sustained-fire standout, but static RPM alone understates it.** 600-round cell, nominal hit=24, caliber=2.4, base 550 RPM. FastAuto has a scripted ramp-up maximum of 1.81818, implying about 1000 RPM once ramped. Its fast mode has wider dispersion (0.0009 versus 0.00037) and a speed multiplier. Mass=450 and base magazine velocity=350 m/s are substantial drawbacks. Its base typicalSpeed=500 means nominal hit=24 is not a guaranteed 24 at impact. Test fast mode and backpack behavior before tuning it.

5. **Marksman contains several distinct power levels.** MK has hit=24, 8 rounds, ~350 RPM semi-auto limit, dispersion=0.00015; its four-round penetrator keeps hit=24 but raises caliber from 2.4 to 20. BR-77 has hit=20, caliber=4, 15 rounds and tighter dispersion=0.000075, but only ~92 RPM. A310's single-shot dispersion=0.000035 is even tighter, with a slower 0.8-second cycle and a three-shot burst alternative. DW-32S's current ammo has hit=10, caliber=1, 20 rounds, dispersion=0.00087. That makes DW-32S a candidate for an upward adjustment or a clearer handling advantage, not a reason to nerf every Marksman weapon together.

6. **CQC is not a single damage scale.** SX-21 has a 12-round cell and a 0.096-second semi-auto cycle (~625 RPM theoretical ceiling), launching six subprojectiles per shot. DP-23 uses ten subprojectiles and a 0.5-second cycle; DC-23 uses twelve and a 0.6-second cycle. Pellet spread, recoil and animation restrictions need live testing before declaring a winner; do not add carrier hit to every pellet. SX-21 is the first burst-output candidate to test. T-14 gives 900 RPM with 50/100-round cells but only nominal hit=5; it favors volume. EE-4 uses the same 18-hit ammo as Rifleman's EE-3 but wider dispersion, so its advantage is automatic close-range handling, not exclusive damage.

7. **Some free rifles compete with paid roles.** A295 uses nominal hit=25, 20 rounds, ~632 RPM full auto and mass=30; compare it with the other JMS rifles under identical armor/velocity conditions. JMS EE-3's 18-hit ammo and 0.0003 dispersion give it a precision niche while remaining Rifleman. The free E-22 and DC-15A have hit=13, 60 rounds and 750 RPM; the paid DC-15LE uses the same damage tier but 40 rounds and 300 RPM, compensated by tighter dispersion. DC-15LE deserves an identity review.

8. **Our reduced HPB is no longer the obvious heavy damage monster.** Its actual Standard muzzle is ~857 RPM (0.07-second cycle), 150 rounds, hit=4.5, caliber=2, dispersion=0.00102. Do not use the unused parent's 600 RPM for this comparison. Heavy/scatter muzzle is ~171 RPM, ten rounds, with our reduced carrier/pellets. It is much heavier than M-41 (275 versus 80 mass); check whether the scatter option and handling justify that cost. I would not reduce HPB damage further before testing it.

## Other specialization conclusions

- **AT/AA:** HH-12 offers both AT and AA cells. Its AT warhead has nominal hit=800 plus a Titan-AT penetrator; IDA PLX has hit=550 plus a Titan-AT penetrator. Those outer hit numbers do not establish an anti-armor winner: guidance, penetrator path and target armor matter. RPS-6 uses CBA disposable-launcher handling, so its fake magazine is not evidence of missing ammunition. No reliable all-target winner established in this pass.
- **CSW:** Mortar, E-Web and autonomous AT/AA turrets serve different targets and deployment roles. They were not given a numeric winner here; a proper comparison requires deployed turret configs, ammo, crew workload and target tests. Do not compare a packed mortar item directly to a rifle.
- **SUAS, Rising Phoenix, Lancer, Communications, Medic and Demolitions:** These purchase drone/mobility/support/explosive capabilities, not interchangeable primary rifles. No primary-weapon balance ranking applies. Jetpack, drone and placed-explosive balance are outside this infantry-weapon stat comparison.

## QM clarity

The QM is descriptive guidance rather than a per-player weapon-class permission map. It names families rather than every classname. Two DLT-19 providers and two FWMB variants exist; avoid treating them as identical. Several LFP Westar carbine variants are in the arsenal but are not explicitly named in the Rifleman wording; their intended assignment should be confirmed. Sidearms were intentionally excluded from QM tracking and are not classified as omissions here. EE-2 high-power and Westar-35S scatter deserve explicit policy decisions.

## Recommended order

1. Decide the permitted EE-2 high-power / Westar scatter roles.
2. Test NT-242 and Bowcaster against the usual armored infantry and a light vehicle.
3. Test Rancor fast mode and SX-21 rapid follow-up against same-role alternatives.
4. Test DW-32S, DC-15LE and HPB for underperformance before applying broad nerfs.
5. Capture an in-game merged config export for LFP and final magazine-well compatibility, then repeat after the planned LS rebalance.

Use the same target class, armor, hit location and range (e.g. 25/100/300/600 m), with multiple shots/trials. Record incapacitation/kill reliability, reload time, recoil recovery and ammunition spent. Keep ACE settings and attachments identical. This review recommends priorities, not automatic stat edits.
